# QB Pro full-site redesign

## Goal
Rebuild the complete QB Pro website in the visual and interaction language of the uploaded recording, while keeping QB Pro’s real services, pricing, work, company details, and contact flow.

## What will change
- Replace the current sparse wordmark-first opening with a clear, centered value proposition explaining web, mobile, backend, cloud, and AI delivery immediately.
- Apply the reference palette across the site: warm cream canvas, near-black typography, white content surfaces, and bright green highlights.
- Rebuild the header as a compact horizontal navigation with a prominent contact action and matching mobile menu.
- Restructure the homepage around the customer journey: offer, services, proof, process, pricing, project updates, FAQ, and contact.
- Restyle portfolio, products, research, services, process, about, FAQ, contact, and legal pages with the same spacing, rounded media, typography, controls, and footer system.
- Keep QB Pro project imagery and existing written claims; no reference brand names or charity content will remain.
- Preserve the interactive project stacks, but adapt them to the new visual language and smooth motion.

## Motion and interaction
- Use soft section reveals, count-up impact figures, horizontal project carousels, rounded button movement, and calm image transitions modeled on the recording.
- Avoid intro gates or motion that delays visitors from reading the offer.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and maintain touch support.

## Validation
- Check the homepage and major inner pages at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Verify navigation, calls to action, portfolio interactions, FAQ, and contact submission flow.
- Confirm all pages compile cleanly and retain their search metadata.

## Technical details
- Consolidate the new palette and shared motion into semantic global design tokens.
- Rework shared header, page heading, footer, and reusable content patterns first, then migrate each route onto them.
- Keep the existing TanStack routing, Lovable Cloud lead submission, project data, and legal routes unchanged in behavior.
